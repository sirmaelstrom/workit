**Goal:** provide (1) the scheduler that decides which WPs may start (decisions.md D5, D12), parsing WPs from a workshop under the Files field rule; (2) one lane-backend interface with two implementations, `herdr` (driving `scripts/lane.mjs` through its CLI) and `exec` (the portable core: `git worktree` + a detached headless agent), exposed to the agent through `conduct.mjs lane <sub>`, with the backend chosen by location; (3) one recorder, `recordLaneStep`, that turns every lane action's result into a routed outcome (D19.15); (4) the one runtime-exercise reader, by marker (D19.23); and (5) the lane brief template. The model table is WP-01's (`lib/adapters.mjs`, D17); this WP imports it.
